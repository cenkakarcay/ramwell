Ramwell
Memory monitor and cleaner for your Mac's menu bar

Know when your Mac is really low on memory.

Ramwell lives in your menu bar and shows memory pressure, the signal macOS itself uses. When memory runs low, it frees it in one click, or automatically, and tells you exactly how much it freed.

  • Memory at a glance: used, available, compressed, cached files and swap, matching Activity Monitor.
  • A one-hour history and the apps using the most memory.
  • Free Memory in one click, and Auto-Clean when low memory lasts.
  • Finds large files and caches filling your disk. Nothing is deleted: files go to the Trash.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no data collected.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store. Requires macOS 14 or later.
