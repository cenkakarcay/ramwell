Frequently asked questions

I don't see the Ramwell icon in the menu bar.

On macOS 26 and later, new menu bar icons can be hidden until you allow them. Open System Settings › Menu Bar and turn on Ramwell. On a Mac with a notch, a crowded menu bar can also hide icons behind the notch.

Can Ramwell free up memory?

No, and your Mac doesn't need it to. macOS uses spare memory as a file cache and gives it back the moment an app needs it, so high memory use on its own isn't a problem. Ramwell shows memory pressure, which tells you when memory is actually short, and the apps using the most memory. Quitting one you don't need is what helps.

What do Normal, Warning and Critical mean?

They are macOS's own memory pressure levels. Normal means your Mac has plenty of memory. Warning means memory is getting tight and macOS is compressing more of it. Critical means your Mac is short on memory and may slow down; quitting an app you don't need helps most.

How do I make Ramwell start when I log in?

Turn on Settings › General › Launch at login. You can turn it off the same way, and quit Ramwell any time from its menu.

Why does Ramwell ask for access to a folder?

Ramwell only looks inside folders you choose, such as the folder to scan for large files or your caches folder. It moves files to the Trash only after you confirm, so you can always put them back.
