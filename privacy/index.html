Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Ramwell does not collect, transmit or share any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code and no network access.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings, stored in macOS preferences on your Mac.
  • The folders you choose to scan. Ramwell remembers your choice so it can scan them again; it never sends their contents anywhere.
  • A one-hour memory history, kept in memory only while the app runs.

Settings › General › Reset All Settings clears your settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Files

Ramwell never deletes your files on its own. Files you move to the Trash with Ramwell stay in the Trash until you empty it.

Contacting us

If you ask for help through GitHub Issues, what you write is public on GitHub. We use it only to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
